Research

My research focuses on speech perception, perceptual learning, and how listeners adapt to variability in spoken language. I am interested in the cognitive mechanisms of adaptive speech processing and, more broadly, the relationship between speech perception and production.

Current Research

  • Speech Adaptation and Perceptual Learning
    Ongoing research · University of Texas at Arlington

    My current work investigates how experience with variable speech shapes perceptual learning and adaptation.

Selected Previous Project

  • Investigating the Impact of Contextual Semantic Information on L2 Garden Path Sentence Comprehension
    Thesis project · Syracuse University · Faculty mentor: Dr. Amanda Brown

    This project examined how native speakers and second-language learners use contextual information while processing garden path sentences.
