Hello, I am Jiaxing Lin. I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Linguistics at the University of Texas at Arlington (UTA). This is my personal academic website for research, teaching, publications, and projects.

My research interests lie in speech perception, perceptual learning, and phonetic adaptation. I am especially interested in how experience with variable speech shapes the way listeners process speech and the cognitive mechanisms that support these adjustments.

More broadly, I am interested in the relationship between speech perception and production, particularly how perceptual learning may influence subsequent speech production. My work draws on experimental psycholinguistics and acoustic approaches to speech.
